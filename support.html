Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Permanent unlocks (Remove Ads, category unlocks) come back on the same Apple ID or Google Play account.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?
No — as long as you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your progress is restored automatically on the same platform.

How do lives work?
You have up to 5 lives. A wrong answer or a timeout costs one, and one life refills every 30 minutes.

How do I delete my data?
Email contact@math-maker.com — see section 8 of our Privacy Policy.
