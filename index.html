How the game works

Four choices.

One answer.

Twenty seconds.

Every level is a single question. A right answer takes you to the next level; a wrong one costs a life. You have five lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes.

04 · The journey

500 levels. Three medals.

Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers in every category. Finish a tier and the badge is yours; across seven categories you graduate from Primary School to Professor.

A chest every 10 levels

Chests open as you advance on the achievements map: Sigma, Epsilon and a power-up every 50 levels. Click the chest.

Daily gift

Every 24 hours, pick one of nine boxes: Epsilon, a power-up or a life. Tap the boxes.
