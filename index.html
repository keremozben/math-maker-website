How to Play

One level is 20 questions. Four choices, one right answer.

1
Pick a Category
Spin the category wheel and tap an unlocked mathematician to start the next level.
2
Answer Fast
You have 20 seconds per question. A wrong answer or a timeout costs a life — lives refill over time.
3
Level Up
Collect Sigma and Epsilon, climb from Bronze to Silver to Gold and unlock the next mind.

Sigma per correct answer = category base × tier (Bronze ×1 · Silver ×1.35 · Gold ×1.75) × time bonus (up to ×2) × streak (×2) × 100
